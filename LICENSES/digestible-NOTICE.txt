digestible (cpp/include/digestible/digestible.h)

digestible is a C++ implementation of the t-digest algorithm, derived from
the t-digest work of Ted Dunning (https://github.com/tdunning/t-digest).
Its header states: "Licensed to Ted Dunning under one or more contributor
license agreements ... under the Apache License, Version 2.0".
Upstream repository: https://github.com/SpirentOrion/digestible

Neither digestible nor tdunning/t-digest ships a NOTICE file (checked 2026-09-29); this
notice records the attribution carried in the source header. The licence
text is in Apache-2.0.txt.
